QUERY 1:
  "What is the price for 1/2 inch Grade 5 hex bolts, and what discount does
   Acme Corporation get?"

EXPECTED RESPONSE:
  The list price for 1/2"-13 x 2" Grade 5 Hex Bolts (SKU: FB-HX5-0500) is
  $0.58 per unit, or $24.65 per case of 50 units.
  [Source: product_catalog.md, Chunk 1]

  Acme Corporation receives a 22% discount on Industrial Fasteners per their
  Platinum contract (MC-2024-AC-001), bringing the per-unit price down to
  approximately $0.45. Note that Acme's fastener orders require a minimum of
  $500 per order to qualify for the contract discount.
  [Source: contract_acme_corp.md, Chunk 2]

QUERY 2:
  "What are the payment terms for Initech Solutions, and do they get an
   early payment discount?"

EXPECTED RESPONSE:
  Initech Solutions (contract MC-2024-IS-003) has Net 30 payment terms from
  invoice date. They do not receive an early payment discount at their Silver
  contract tier. Their credit limit is $12,000. Accepted payment methods
  include ACH, corporate check, and procurement card for orders under $2,500.
  [Source: contract_initech_solutions.md, Chunk 5]

  For comparison, Acme Corporation (Platinum tier) has Net 60 terms with a
  2/15 early payment discount (2% if paid within 15 days), and Globex
  Industries (Gold tier) has Net 45 terms with a 1/10 early payment discount
  (1% if paid within 10 days).
  [Source: contract_acme_corp.md, Chunk 6]
  [Source: contract_globex_industries.md, Chunk 5]

  Riverside manufacturing facility (Dock 7):

  - Nitrile Gloves (L), GL-NIT-L: 5,000 units, replenished at 1,500 remaining
  - Nitrile Gloves (XL), GL-NIT-XL: 3,000 units, replenished at 1,000 remaining
  - Cut-Resistant Gloves A4, GL-CUT-A4: 200 pairs, replenished at 50 remaining
  - Clear Safety Glasses, SG-CL-100: 120 units, replenished at 30 remaining
  - 1/2" Grade 5 Hex Bolts, FB-HX5-0500: 500 units, replenished at 100 remaining
  [Source: contract_acme_corp.md, Chunk 5]

  inventory counts are performed quarterly. Acme is responsible for
  shrinkage/loss above a 2% threshold.
  [Source: contract_acme_corp.md, Chunk 5]

QUERY 4:
  "Can I return opened safety glasses? What is the return policy?"

EXPECTED RESPONSE:
  No, opened safety equipment (including hard hats, safety glasses, and
  gloves) cannot be returned due to the hygiene policy. This applies to all
  customers.
  [Source: shipping_policies.md, Chunk 10]
  [Source: product_catalog.md, Chunk 11]

  For unopened safety equipment, standard returns are accepted within 30
  days of delivery with a 15% restocking fee. All returns require a Return
  Merchandise Authorization (RMA) number, which can be requested via phone,
  email, or the customer portal.
  [Source: shipping_policies.md, Chunk 9]
