## 6. Shipping and Handling

- **Standard Shipping:** 5-7 business days, free on orders over $500
- **Expedited Shipping:** 2-3 business days, $25 flat rate or 5% of order value (whichever is greater)
- **Next-Day Shipping:** 1 business day, $45 flat rate or 8% of order value (whichever is greater)
- **Freight / LTL:** For orders over 150 lbs or oversized items; quoted per shipment
- **Hazmat Surcharge:** $15 per shipment for items containing hazardous materials (welding gas, certain chemicals)

## 7. Returns and Warranty

- **Standard Return Policy:** 30 days from delivery, restocking fee of 15% on opened items
- **Defective Products:** Full refund or replacement within 90 days, no restocking fee
- **Power Tool Warranty:** Manufacturer's warranty (typically 3-5 years), handled through manufacturer
- **Custom / Cut-to-Length Items:** Non-returnable
- **Safety Equipment:** Non-returnable once packaging is opened (hygiene policy)
