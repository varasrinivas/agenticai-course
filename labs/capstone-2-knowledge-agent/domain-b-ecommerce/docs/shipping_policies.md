# Meridian Industrial Supply — Shipping, Handling, and Logistics Policies

**Policy Version:** 2024.1
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024
**Last Updated:** December 20, 2023

---

## 1. Shipping Methods and Transit Times

### 1.1 Standard Ground Shipping

- **Carrier:** FastFreight Logistics (primary), Regional Express (secondary)
- **Transit Time:** 5-7 business days from ship date
- **Cutoff Time:** Orders placed by 2 PM EST ship same day (in-stock items)
- **Tracking:** Provided via email upon shipment; available in customer portal
- **Cost:**
  - Orders under $500: Flat rate $12.95 or actual freight cost (whichever is higher)
  - Orders $500 - $999: Flat rate $8.95
  - Orders $1,000+: Free standard shipping
  - Contract customers: See individual contract terms

### 1.2 Expedited Shipping (2-3 Business Days)

- **Carrier:** FastFreight Express or UPS 2-Day
- **Transit Time:** 2-3 business days from ship date
- **Cutoff Time:** Orders placed by 12 PM EST ship same day
- **Cost:** $25 flat rate or 5% of order value (whichever is greater)
- **Availability:** Continental US only (excludes Alaska and Hawaii)
- **Restrictions:** Not available for freight/LTL shipments or hazmat items

### 1.3 Next-Day Shipping

- **Carrier:** UPS Next Day Air or FedEx Priority Overnight
- **Transit Time:** 1 business day from ship date
- **Cutoff Time:** Orders placed by 10 AM EST ship same day
- **Cost:** $45 flat rate or 8% of order value (whichever is greater)
- **Availability:** Continental US metro areas; delivery to rural areas may take an additional day
- **Restrictions:** Not available for orders exceeding 70 lbs per package, freight/LTL shipments, or hazmat items

### 1.4 Freight / LTL (Less Than Truckload)

- **When Required:** Orders exceeding 150 lbs total weight, palletized shipments, or oversized items
- **Carriers:** FastFreight Logistics, YRC Freight, XPO Logistics
- **Transit Time:** 3-10 business days depending on origin/destination
- **Cost:** Quoted per shipment based on weight, dimensions, freight class, and destination
- **Minimum Charge:** $95
- **Liftgate Service:** Additional $75 if delivery location does not have a loading dock
- **Inside Delivery:** Additional $125 for delivery beyond the receiving dock
- **Appointment Delivery:** Additional $50 for scheduled delivery window

## 2. Shipping Zones and Coverage

### 2.1 Warehouse Locations

Meridian ships from three distribution centers:

| DC Code | Location | Coverage Area |
|---------|----------|---------------|
| DC-EAST | Harrisburg, PA | Northeast, Mid-Atlantic, Southeast |
| DC-CENT | Chicago, IL | Midwest, Great Plains, South Central |
| DC-WEST | Reno, NV | Mountain West, Pacific Northwest, Southwest |

Orders are fulfilled from the DC closest to the delivery address. Split
shipments may occur if inventory is distributed across multiple DCs.

### 2.2 Split Shipments

- If an order contains items stocked at different DCs, items may ship separately
- Customer is not charged additional shipping for split shipments
- Each shipment receives its own tracking number
- Backorder items ship separately when they become available

### 2.3 International Shipping

- Meridian does not currently ship outside the United States
- For international requirements, contact your account manager for partner referrals

## 3. Hazardous Materials Shipping

### 3.1 Hazmat Products

The following products in our catalog are classified as hazardous materials:

| Product Category | Hazard Class | UN Number | Proper Shipping Name |
|-----------------|-------------|-----------|---------------------|
| Compressed Gas (Welding) | 2.2 (Non-flammable gas) | UN1006 (Argon), UN1013 (CO2) | Argon, compressed / Carbon dioxide |
| Aerosol Products | 2.1 (Flammable gas) | UN1950 | Aerosols |
| Certain Adhesives/Sealants | 3 (Flammable liquid) | UN1133 | Adhesives |
| Lithium Batteries (in power tools) | 9 (Miscellaneous) | UN3481 | Lithium ion batteries contained in equipment |

### 3.2 Hazmat Shipping Rules

- **Surcharge:** $15 per shipment containing hazardous materials
- **Documentation:** Safety Data Sheet (SDS) included with every hazmat shipment
- **Labeling:** All hazmat packages labeled per DOT 49 CFR regulations
- **Carrier Restrictions:** Hazmat items cannot ship via air (Next-Day or Expedited Air); ground only
- **Quantity Limits:** Per DOT limited quantity and excepted quantity exemptions where applicable
- **Customer Responsibility:** Receiving facility must have appropriate storage and handling capabilities

## 4. Packaging Standards

### 4.1 Standard Packaging

- All items packed in corrugated cartons rated for the shipped weight
- Fragile items receive foam cushioning or bubble wrap
- Heavy items (>50 lbs per carton) shipped on pallets
- Maximum single carton weight: 70 lbs

### 4.2 Special Packaging

| Item Type | Packaging Method | Additional Cost |
|-----------|-----------------|-----------------|
| Stainless Steel Fasteners | VCI (vapor corrosion inhibitor) paper | Included |
| Welding Wire (>25 lb spools) | Palletized, stretch-wrapped | Included |
| Safety Glasses (bulk) | Individually bagged, master carton | Included |
| Power Tools | Manufacturer packaging within outer carton | Included |
| Compressed Gas Cylinders | DOT-certified cylinder, secured upright on pallet | $25/cylinder |

## 5. Delivery Procedures

### 5.1 Receiving Requirements

- **Inspection:** Customer should inspect all packages for visible damage upon receipt
- **Signature:** Signature required for orders over $500 and all hazmat deliveries
- **Damage Reporting:**
  - Note damage on the carrier's delivery receipt BEFORE signing
  - Report damage to Meridian within 48 hours of delivery
  - Photograph damaged items and packaging
  - Do not discard damaged packaging until claim is resolved
- **Shortage Reporting:** Report any missing items within 5 business days of delivery

### 5.2 Delivery Attempts

- **Standard:** Up to 3 delivery attempts for small parcel shipments
- **Freight:** 1 delivery attempt; redelivery fee of $75 if initial delivery fails due to customer unavailability
- **Address Correction:** $15 fee for carrier-initiated address corrections

### 5.3 Proof of Delivery

- Available in the customer portal within 24 hours of delivery
- Includes: delivery date/time, signature image (if applicable), GPS coordinates
- Historical POD records available for 24 months

## 6. Returns and Exchanges

### 6.1 Return Authorization

All returns require a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) number:

- Request via phone, email, or customer portal
- RMA issued within 2 business days (1 business day for Platinum accounts)
- RMA valid for 14 days from issue date
- Ship returns to the DC specified on the RMA (may differ from originating DC)

### 6.2 Return Shipping

| Return Reason | Return Shipping Paid By | Restocking Fee |
|--------------|------------------------|----------------|
| Defective / damaged | Meridian | None |
| Incorrect item shipped | Meridian | None |
| Customer order error | Customer | 15% |
| Overstock / no longer needed | Customer | 15% |
| Refused delivery (no valid reason) | Customer | 15% + original shipping |

### 6.3 Non-Returnable Items

- Custom or made-to-order products
- Cut-to-length items (wire, rope, chain)
- Opened safety equipment (hard hats, glasses, gloves) — hygiene policy
- Compressed gas cylinders (contact gas supplier for returns)
- Electrical components that have been installed
- Items not in original, resalable condition

### 6.4 Credit Processing

- Credit issued within 10 business days of receiving and inspecting returned goods
- Credits applied to customer account; refund checks issued upon request (allow 30 days)
- Defective item credits include original shipping charges

## 7. Order Tracking and Notifications

### 7.1 Automated Notifications

Customers receive email notifications at each stage:

1. **Order Received** — confirmation with order number and expected ship date
2. **Order Processing** — order picked and packed, any backorder items noted
3. **Shipped** — tracking number(s), carrier, estimated delivery date
4. **Delivered** — delivery confirmation with POD link
5. **Backorder Update** — estimated availability date (sent weekly until fulfilled)

### 7.2 Customer Portal

The customer portal (meridian-supply.example.com) provides:

- Real-time order status and tracking
- Order history (24 months)
- Invoice and payment history
- RMA status
- Product availability and pricing (contract-specific)
- Reorder from previous orders
- Usage reports and analytics

## 8. Claims and Disputes

### 8.1 Freight Claims

- Damage claims must be filed within 48 hours of delivery
- Meridian handles all carrier freight claims on behalf of the customer
- Credit or replacement issued within 5 business days of claim approval
- For visible damage: note on delivery receipt, photograph, and report

### 8.2 Pricing Disputes

- Report pricing discrepancies within 30 days of invoice date
- Meridian will research and respond within 3 business days
- Credits issued within 5 business days if pricing error confirmed
- Contract pricing takes precedence over catalog pricing in all cases
