## 1. Contract Summary

This agreement establishes the terms and conditions between Meridian Industrial
Supply ("Supplier") and Globex Industries, Inc. ("Customer") for the
procurement of industrial supplies and safety equipment.

## 5. Payment Terms

- **Standard Terms:** Net 45 from invoice date
- **Early Payment Discount:** 1% discount if paid within 10 days (1/10 Net 45)
- **Late Payment:** 1.5% monthly interest on balances past due
- **Credit Limit:** $30,000
- **Payment Methods:** ACH, wire transfer, corporate check, procurement card (orders < $5,000)
- **Invoice Delivery:** Email PDF

## 6. Shipping Terms

- **Standard Shipping:** Prepaid and added to invoice; free on orders over $750
- **FOB Point:** FOB Shipping Point (risk transfers at shipment)
- **Delivery Locations:**
  - Globex HQ & Warehouse: 3500 Technology Drive, Shelbyville, IN 46176
  - Globex West: 1100 Pacific Avenue, Portland, OR 97201

## 7. Quality Requirements

- All products must meet stated industry standards
- Certificates of Conformance available upon request (48-hour turnaround)
- Material Test Reports for stainless steel orders over $2,000
- Non-conforming product: RMA issued within 48 hours

## 8. Contract Renewal and Termination

- **Auto-Renewal:** This contract auto-renews for 1-year terms unless either party provides 60 days' written notice
- **Termination for Convenience:** Either party may terminate with 60 days' written notice
- **Termination for Cause:** 30 days' notice with 15 days to cure
