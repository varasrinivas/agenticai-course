# Customer Contract — Acme Corporation

**Contract Number:** MC-2024-AC-001
**Customer:** Acme Corporation
**Account Type:** Platinum
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024
**Expiration Date:** December 31, 2025
**Payment Terms:** Net 60
**Annual Committed Spend:** $180,000

---

## 1. Contract Summary

This agreement establishes the terms and conditions between Meridian Industrial
Supply ("Supplier") and Acme Corporation ("Customer") for the procurement of
industrial supplies, safety equipment, and related products. This contract
supersedes all prior agreements between the parties.

## 2. Pricing Schedule

### 2.1 Category-Specific Discounts

Acme Corporation receives the following category-specific discounts off
standard list prices:

| Product Category | Discount | Minimum Order Qty |
|-----------------|----------|-------------------|
| Industrial Fasteners | 22% | $500 per order |
| Safety Equipment (Hard Hats, Glasses) | 18% | No minimum |
| Gloves (all types) | 20% | 1 case minimum |
| Power Tools | 12% | No minimum |
| Welding Supplies (Wire & Gas) | 15% | $300 per order |
| Stainless Steel Fasteners | 20% | $750 per order |

### 2.2 Price Protection

- Contract prices are guaranteed for the duration of the contract term
- Price increases are limited to a maximum of 3% per calendar year, with
  90 days' written notice
- Price decreases due to market conditions will be passed through within
  30 days
- Raw material surcharges (steel, nickel) may be applied with 60 days'
  notice if commodity prices increase by more than 15% from baseline

### 2.3 Annual Volume Rebate

In addition to the category discounts, Acme receives a year-end volume rebate
based on total annual purchases:

| Annual Spend | Rebate |
|-------------|--------|
| $100,000 - $149,999 | 1.0% |
| $150,000 - $199,999 | 1.5% |
| $200,000 - $299,999 | 2.0% |
| $300,000+ | 2.5% |

Rebate is calculated on total net purchases (after discounts, before tax and
freight) and issued as a credit memo in January of the following year.

## 5. Payment Terms

- **Standard Terms:** Net 60 from invoice date
- **Early Payment Discount:** 2% discount if paid within 15 days (2/15 Net 60)
- **Late Payment:** 1.5% monthly interest on balances past due
- **Credit Limit:** $75,000
- **Payment Methods:** ACH (preferred), wire transfer, corporate check
- **Invoice Delivery:** Electronic (EDI 810 or email PDF)
- **Disputed Invoices:** Must be reported within 30 days of invoice date; undisputed portion must be paid by due date

## 6. Shipping Terms

- **Standard Shipping:** Prepaid and added to invoice (included in orders over $1,000)
- **FOB Point:** FOB Destination (Supplier retains risk until delivery)
- **Preferred Carrier:** FastFreight Logistics (account #AC-88421)
- **Delivery Locations:**
  - Acme HQ: 1200 Industrial Blvd, Springfield, IL 62704
  - Acme Riverside Plant: 800 River Road, Riverside, CA 92501
  - Acme Southeast DC: 450 Commerce Way, Atlanta, GA 30301
- **Drop Ship:** Available to Acme customer sites at standard shipping rates + $5 handling fee

## 7. Quality Requirements

- All products must meet stated ANSI, ASTM, or ISO standards
- Certificates of Conformance (CoC) required for all fastener orders over $1,000
- Material Test Reports (MTR) required for all stainless steel orders
- First article inspection may be requested for new products (5 business day turnaround)
- Non-conforming product: Supplier to issue RMA within 24 hours of notification

## 8. Contract Renewal and Termination

- **Auto-Renewal:** This contract auto-renews for successive 1-year terms unless either party provides 90 days' written notice
- **Termination for Convenience:** Either party may terminate with 90 days' written notice
- **Termination for Cause:** Immediate termination for material breach, with 30 days to cure for non-material breach
- **Post-Termination:** Open orders will be fulfilled at contract pricing; consignment inventory will be reconciled within 60 days
