[ASSISTANT] A UCC-1 filing is a legal document filed under the Uniform
Commercial Code that puts the public on notice that a creditor (the "secured
party") has a security interest in specific assets of a debtor. When a lender
makes a loan secured by collateral -- such as equipment, inventory, or accounts
receivable -- they file a UCC-1 financing statement with the appropriate state
office (usually the Secretary of State) to "perfect" their security interest.

legally recognized priority claim on the collateral ahead of unsecured creditors.
